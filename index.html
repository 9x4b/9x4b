FAQ

Do you hold my funds?

No. Kestrel is non-custodial. Trades are signed by your own wallet, and we recommend a dedicated burner wallet.

Does the rug check guarantee safety?

No. Checks reduce risk but can't eliminate it. Many tokens that pass can still lose value or be manipulated.

Which chains are supported?

Solana, Base and BNB Chain. More can be added.

How fast is execution?

Speed depends on network congestion, your priority fee and RPC. No result is guaranteed.
